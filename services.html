  1. Email us with your restaurant concept, country / city, and what you are trying to decide.

  3. Quote first. You see the price and delivery time before deciding.

About the consultant
Licensed chef in Japan (chōrishi) with about 15 years of experience in the food and beverage industry, specializing in Japanese cuisine (washoku). Experience covers daily kitchen work, menu planning and the practical side of running food service in Japan.

What this service is not

This is practical menu and operations advice based on experience in Japan. It is not legal, tax, licensing, food-safety certification or financial advice for your country. Local permits and rules must be confirmed with your local authorities and professionals.
